Method and dataset audit

Protocol

  1. One frozen dataset: 200 FLORES-200 sentence pairs, 200 Belebele reading questions and 30 grammar probes.
  2. Inputs and generated prompts are checked against SHA-256 hashes before every run.
  3. Gemini runs through the Google Gemini API. Qwen 2.5 1.5B and Gemma 3 run locally through Ollama.
  4. Every response is saved with its prompt hash, settings hash, model version and timestamp. Reference answers never enter the prompt.
  5. The scorer computes QA accuracy, corpus chrF2, the translation gap and paired QA tests with Holm correction.
